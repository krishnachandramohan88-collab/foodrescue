THE PROBLEM

Good Food Is Lost While People Still Need It.

The challenge is not only food waste. The real challenge is rescuing usable food at the right time.

🍱

Surplus Food

Restaurants, hotels and events can have prepared food left over after demand changes.

⏰

Limited Time

Safe redistribution is time-sensitive. Waiting too long can turn usable food into waste.

🔗

Disconnected Network

Food businesses, NGOs, donors and people in need are often not connected through one rescue workflow.
