THE PROBLEM

Usable food is wasted because rescue decisions happen too late.

Restaurants, hotels and events may have surplus prepared food, but NGOs and delivery partners often receive incomplete or delayed information.

01

Surplus Food

02

Limited Time

03

Disconnected Network

Restaurants, NGOs, delivery partners and communities lack one coordinated workflow.
